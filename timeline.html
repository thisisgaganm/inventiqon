MILESTONE SEQUENCE / 2026

FROM FIRST SIGNAL
TO FINAL DEMO

Key dates along the INVENTIQON journey. The onboarding date remains unconfirmed; precise event timings will be shared by the organizers.

  1. 14 OCTOBER 2026

    REGISTRATION OPENS

    Team registration begins. Form a team of exactly three eligible members and submit your details through the official registration form.

    01 / REGISTRATION

  2. 10 NOVEMBER 2026

    REGISTRATION CLOSES

    Submit your team's registration before the announced closing date. Late registrations are not accepted unless the organizers officially announce otherwise.

    02 / SUBMISSION

  3. 13 NOVEMBER 2026

    SHORTLISTING & PAYMENT PROCESS

    Shortlisted teams are announced and receive the relevant payment instructions. Follow the official communication from the organizing team.

    03 / TEAM CONFIRMATION

  4. 15 NOVEMBER 2026

    PAYMENT CONFIRMATION DEADLINE

    Complete the required payment and team-detail confirmation according to the official instructions.

    04 / PAYMENT

  5. DATE TO BE CONFIRMED

    TEAM ONBOARDING

    Watch for official onboarding instructions, event guidelines, and any updates shared by the organizers.

    05 / ONBOARDING

  6. 19 NOVEMBER 2026

    INVENTIQON BEGINS

    Arrive at the designated venue, complete the announced check-in process, and prepare to begin the on-campus hardware challenge.

    06 / DAY ONE

  7. 19–20 NOVEMBER 2026

    36-HOUR HARDWARE CHALLENGE

    Design, build, debug, optimize, and test a functional hardware prototype within the official event window.

    07 / BUILD WINDOW

  8. 20 NOVEMBER 2026

    FINAL DEMONSTRATION & EVALUATION

    Present your working prototype and explain its innovation, feasibility, relevance, and potential impact to the judging panel. The precise schedule will be announced by the organizers.

    08 / FINAL REVIEW
