VidyutSpanda is an international-level hardware innovation event hosted by Dayananda Sagar University, Bengaluru. It brings together students, innovators and technologists to conceptualize, design and build functional prototypes that address real-world challenges through technology and creativity.

06 / 06

Build protective electronics with secure circuit-board layouts, fault monitoring and resilient hardware safeguards.

FROM CONCEPT TO HARDWARE

IDEATE. ENGINEER.
BUILD. PROVE.

A focused path from proposal to working prototype, with shortlisting before the on-campus build round.

  1. 01

    Ideate

    Teams develop a solution concept and present their proposed approach through a PPT submission.

  2. 02

    Get shortlisted

    Proposals are evaluated by industry experts and academic mentors. Shortlisted teams proceed to the on-campus prototype round.

  3. 03

    Build

    Shortlisted teams develop a functional hardware prototype during the 36-hour event window.

  4. 04

    Demonstrate

    Teams present their working prototypes and explain their innovation, feasibility, real-world relevance and impact.
