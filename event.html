EVENT OVERVIEW

WHERE ENGINEERING
BECOMES REAL

INVENTIQON is an international-level innovation event hosted by Dayananda Sagar University, Bengaluru. It is designed to bring together visionary students, innovators and technologists from across India and abroad.

The event encourages participants to conceptualize, design and build functional prototypes that address real-world challenges through technology and creativity.

Participants explore Industry 5.0, embedded intelligence, robotics, sustainable energy, healthcare technology and next-generation hardware. Guidance from industry mentors and academic experts is subject to the organizers' confirmed arrangements; the event promotes practical engineering, interdisciplinary collaboration and technology-driven social impact.

SIX TECHNOLOGY DOMAINS

CHOOSE YOUR
TECHNOLOGY FRONTIER

Six directions for turning an engineering challenge into working hardware.

01 / 06

Industry 5.0 & Intelligent Automation

Focused on human-centric smart manufacturing and automation. Projects could include collaborative robots, predictive maintenance systems, IoT-enabled factory monitoring and intelligent process control that make industry safer, more efficient and more sustainable.

02 / 06

Edge AI & Deep Tech

For teams building AI that runs directly on devices instead of relying entirely on the cloud. This track includes TinyML, real-time computer vision, sensor-driven intelligence and low-power embedded AI solutions, emphasizing speed, privacy and efficiency.

03 / 06

Robotics, Autonomous Systems & Smart Mobility

For teams working on robots, drones and self-navigating systems. This track includes service and industrial robots, autonomous vehicles, swarm robotics and intelligent transport solutions, emphasizing hardware design and AI-driven autonomy.

04 / 06

Smart Energy & Climate Resilience

Focused on technology for a sustainable future. Projects could include smart grids, solar and battery management, energy monitoring, air and water quality sensing, and early-warning systems for climate and disaster challenges.

05 / 06

HealthTech & Assistive Devices

Focused on innovations that improve healthcare and accessibility. Projects could include wearable health monitors, rehabilitation aids, assistive tools for people with disabilities and affordable diagnostic devices that improve quality of life.

06 / 06

Next-Gen Hardware & Smart Devices

For teams building new and unconventional hardware. This track includes custom PCB designs, smart consumer devices, wireless and IoT products, and creative embedded systems that push the limits of compact hardware.

PROJECT REQUIREMENTS & CONDUCT

BUILD ORIGINAL.
BUILD FUNCTIONAL.

Review the supplied participation, prototype and conduct requirements before preparing a submission.

01 / TEAM

Team and participation

  • Teams must have exactly three members.
  • Teams must follow the official registration and participation requirements.
  • Participants must comply with event and university regulations.

02 / PROTOTYPE

Prototype requirements

  • Teams must demonstrate a functional hardware prototype.
  • Software-only submissions or simulation results alone are insufficient.
  • Participants must bring their own laptops, development boards, components, sensors and required tools.
  • Projects must be built and demonstrated within the official event window.

03 / ORIGINAL WORK

Original work

  • Pre-defined or previously developed code is prohibited under the supplied rules.
  • Pre-built modules are not permitted, except standard sensors and microcontrollers.
  • Plagiarism, misconduct or rule violations can result in disqualification.

04 / ATTENDANCE

Attendance and submission

  • Teams must remain at the venue throughout the 36-hour duration.
  • At least one team member must be present at all times.
  • Late submissions are not accepted.
  • All required deliverables must be submitted according to organizer instructions.

05 / CONDUCT

Judging and conduct

  • The judges' and organizing committee's decisions are final and binding.
  • Participants must maintain professional conduct.
  • Failure to comply with official instructions may lead to disqualification.

JUDGING FRAMEWORK

HOW YOUR PROTOTYPE
WILL BE EVALUATED

Prototypes are considered across five dimensions. No numerical weightages are published here.

01

Innovation

Originality, creativity and uniqueness of the idea.

02

Feasibility

Practical implementation, engineering quality and potential scalability.

03

ROI and ROP Impact

Projects demonstrating clear ROI and strong ROP impact receive additional weightage according to the supplied event brief.

Organizer clarification: ROP is retained as supplied; its intended meaning must be confirmed before publication.

04

Relevance

Alignment with the selected theme, problem statement and real-world impact.

05

Presentation

Clarity, technical explanation, demonstration quality and effective communication.
